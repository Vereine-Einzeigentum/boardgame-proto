# Obscur — Card Library (rebuild v1)

**Authority:** built on `OBSCUR-DECISIONS.md` (the locked model) over `OBSCUR-SPEC.md`
(the floor). Decisions here were weighted for **engagement x profitability**, which
CTA-v1 Phase 1 classified as a **dialectic** (congruence 0.77) — so every card is meant to
be fun *and* to feed a monetization surface through the same mechanic, never one at the
other's expense. Open verbs / economy were chosen with a wayfinder matrix (see the commit
message / session notes).

## The model (locked)

- **Mask card = a bonus (activated, sometimes passive) + a passive penalty.** Both ride on
  the card, permanent once drafted. The passive penalty *is* the handicap (G8).
- **Two mask cards per tier:** **a = primary** archetype (modest bonus, modest penalty);
  **b = shadow** archetype (bigger bonus, bigger penalty — the inverted-tarot / Jungian
  shadow, higher risk / higher reward).
- **Private by default.** No card reveals your state (points, Focus, cards, build) to other
  players unless its text explicitly says so — and **very few** do.
- Counts: tiers 0-3; start 3 verbs (2 universal + 1 mask), end 6; >=2 mask cards per tier;
  theme packs add equally to all six masks (G7).

## Terms

- **points** — the score / win currency; game ends, points are counted, one winner.
- **Static** — the shared danger clock; at threshold the House Fractures the road.
- **Focus** — per-turn resource, **2 per turn, no carry**; spent in the Breath.
- **circuit** — one lap of the road.

## Universal ladder (the shared floor)

| Tier | Verb | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 0 start | **WITNESS** | see the board; once/turn log a brag-log token. Free. |
| 0 start | **TETHER** | mark a link to another traveler; no resource moves; later cards (yours or theirs) can exploit the tether. Free. |
| 1 | **BEND** | spend 1 Focus: shift the die +/-1. |
| 2 | **STAKE** | spend 1 Focus: shift the die +/-2, but the swing resolves *after* a coin the House flips — better odds are buyable (see Monetization). |
| 3 | **WREST** | spend 2 Focus: take one action on another traveler *during their Breath* (force/deny a move). The endgame verb of hostility. |

Universal **cards** (drafted) grant the tier verb + a small always-good boost (e.g. +1 Focus
cap that run). They carry **no penalty** — that is the difference from mask cards.

## THORN — REROUTE · defiance (refuse the given, choose the unknown)
*Handicap: compulsive refusal — cannot sit with what the road offers.*

- **T1a Forked Path** *(primary)* — **Bonus (activated):** REROUTE, and see both alternates
  before you commit. **Passive penalty:** the first LIGHT event you land on each circuit
  gives you nothing (you flinch from the easy gift).
- **T1b Thornsworn** *(shadow)* — **Bonus (activated):** REROUTE and swap to any event *type*
  you name, not a blind alternate. **Passive penalty:** every turn you decline to REROUTE
  **when an event is available to redirect**, lose 1 point — standing still rots you.
- **T2a Crooked Mile** *(primary)* — **Bonus (activated):** REROUTE also moves your token to
  the chosen alternate's space. **Passive penalty:** you can never hold BEND or STAKE — you
  only bend the road by refusing it.
- **T2b Bramble Crown** *(shadow)* — **Bonus (activated):** force another traveler's current
  event to become a different event of your choosing; it still resolves on them (hostile).
  **Passive penalty:** each time you do, you also take half of their original event, rounded
  up.
- **T3a The Long Way** *(primary)* — **Bonus (activated):** REROUTE to the event three spaces
  ahead and jump there. **Passive penalty:** you always skip the spaces between — their
  points never count for you.
- **T3b Refusalborn** *(shadow)* — **Bonus (activated):** once per circuit, REROUTE cancels
  **any** event on the board this turn, anyone's. **Passive penalty:** each use costs you
  2 points and adds 1 Static — refusal on this scale bleeds you and the road.

## EMBER — SURGE · recklessness (outrun the road; the table pays)
*Handicap: momentum without brakes — cost lands on others, then on you.*

- **T1a Kindling** *(primary)* — **Bonus (activated):** SURGE up to 3 extra spaces.
  **Passive penalty:** +1 Static per extra space, always.
- **T1b Backdraft** *(shadow)* — **Bonus (activated):** SURGE, and any traveler whose token
  you cross takes 1 Static. **Passive penalty:** if you cross no one on a SURGE, you take
  2 Static yourself.
- **T2a Wildfire** *(primary)* — **Bonus (activated):** SURGE; the next traveler is dragged
  +1 space in your wake. **Passive penalty:** you can never move fewer than 2 spaces on your
  own turn (you can't slow down).
- **T2b Ember's Debt** *(shadow)* — **Bonus (activated):** SURGE +4 now. **Passive penalty:**
  your *next* turn you do not move at all — borrowed against the future, every time.
- **T3a Immolation** *(primary)* — **Bonus (activated):** SURGE to any space in the current
  road segment. **Passive penalty:** Static gained this way is doubled; if it Fractures the
  road you lose 2 points as the named cause.
- **T3b Phoenix** *(shadow)* — **Bonus (activated):** the turn a Fracture lands, SURGE free
  and gain 3 points. **Passive penalty:** any turn *without* a Fracture, you gain no points
  from movement — you only feed on collapse.

## VEIL — SHED · relief (slip the effect, forfeit the gift)
*Handicap: avoidance — safety bought by never receiving.*

- **T1a Gossamer** *(primary)* — **Bonus (activated):** SHED harm but keep any positive part.
  **Passive penalty:** costs 2 Focus (your whole turn's Focus) each use.
- **T1b Second Skin** *(shadow)* — **Bonus (activated):** SHED and refund 1 Focus.
  **Passive penalty:** the first point you would gain each turn is lost — relief numbs
  reward.
- **T2a Mistwalk** *(primary)* — **Bonus (activated):** SHED another traveler's incoming harm
  into nothing (private — the table never learns whose). **Passive penalty:** you gain 1
  Static each use.
- **T2b Hollow** *(shadow)* — **Bonus (activated):** SHED a whole event and become
  untargetable until your next turn. **Passive penalty:** while untargetable you also cannot
  gain points.
- **T3a Vanish** *(primary)* — **Bonus (activated):** SHED yourself off the board for one full
  round; nothing touches you. **Passive penalty:** you take no turns that round.
- **T3b The Unseen** *(shadow)* — **Bonus (passive):** harm you avoid by any means converts to
  points, 1 for 1. **Passive penalty:** you can never win on the exit tile — you can only win
  on points count, never by holding position.

## MOSS — CARRY · tenderness (take the hit for another)
*Handicap: over-giving — protects others into your own ruin.*

- **T1a Cushion** *(primary)* — **Bonus (activated):** CARRY another's harm at half.
  **Passive penalty:** each CARRY also costs you 1 point.
- **T1b Roothold** *(shadow)* — **Bonus (activated):** CARRY and form a hidden tether with the
  one you saved (later exploitable). **Passive penalty:** the turn after any CARRY you cannot
  move.
- **T2a Shelter** *(primary)* — **Bonus (activated):** CARRY for every traveler hit by one
  event, not just one. **Passive penalty:** you take the full summed harm, no reduction.
- **T2b Give Warmth** *(shadow)* — **Bonus (activated):** CARRY and move 1 of your points to
  the saved traveler. **Passive penalty:** you may never hold more points than the traveler
  you last saved — generosity caps you to those you lift (privately known from the transfer).
- **T3a The Whole Weight** *(primary)* — **Bonus (activated):** CARRY every harm on the table
  this turn onto yourself. **Passive penalty:** you take the full summed harm; if it would
  drop you below 0 points you are knocked back to the start of the circuit instead of out.
- **T3b Evergreen** *(shadow)* — **Bonus (passive):** you auto-absorb the first harm to any
  traveler each round, free. **Passive penalty:** each absorb costs you 1 point, and you
  never gain points from LIGHT events — you receive only harm, never the road's gifts.

## MOON — SURVEY · foresight (see ahead; keep it to yourself)
*Handicap: knowing without acting — spends turns on sight.*

- **T1a Half-Light** *(primary)* — **Bonus (activated, private):** SURVEY the next event —
  **you alone** see it. **Passive penalty:** you cannot also move and SURVEY the same turn.
- **T1b Waxing** *(shadow)* — **Bonus (activated, private):** SURVEY the next **three** events,
  yours only. **Passive penalty:** the turn after any SURVEY you must accept the die with no
  BEND/STAKE — foreknowledge freezes the hand.
- **T2a New Moon** *(primary)* — **Bonus (activated, private):** SURVEY, then BEND for free
  this turn using what you saw. **Passive penalty:** the turn you SURVEY you have 1 Focus,
  not 2.
- **T2b Eclipse** *(shadow)* — **Bonus (activated):** SURVEY, and **hide** the next event's
  class from everyone else (they act blind; you don't). **Passive penalty:** +1 Static each
  use — blinding the table strains the road.
- **T3a Tide Table** *(primary)* — **Bonus (activated, private):** SURVEY the whole rest of the
  current segment, yours only. **Passive penalty:** that turn you neither move nor act.
- **T3b The Blind Prophet** *(shadow)* — **Bonus (activated):** once per run, name any event
  and it occurs. **Passive penalty:** you may never SURVEY again after using it — the
  prophecy costs you sight.

## ASH — DECLARE · memory (call it before it lands)
*Handicap: needing to be right — vindication over safety.*

- **T1a Cinders** *(primary)* — **Bonus (activated, private):** privately DECLARE the die
  outcome; if right, +2 points. **Passive penalty:** if wrong, -1 point.
- **T1b Last Word** *(shadow)* — **Bonus (activated):** DECLARE about another traveler's turn;
  right = you place a brag-log token on your log. **Passive penalty:** wrong = they place one
  on *your* log, and the table sees it.
- **T2a The Record** *(primary)* — **Bonus (activated):** DECLARE by copying the last resolved
  event onto yourself. **Passive penalty:** you must accept it even if it harms you.
- **T2b Witness Mark** *(shadow)* — **Bonus (activated):** right DECLARE lets you place 2
  brag-log tokens anywhere. **Passive penalty:** a wrong DECLARE costs you your WITNESS for
  2 turns — silenced.
- **T3a Long Memory** *(primary)* — **Bonus (activated):** DECLARE by re-invoking any event
  from earlier this run onto yourself. **Passive penalty:** each use permanently lowers your
  Focus cap by 1 for the run.
- **T3b Vindication** *(shadow)* — **Bonus (activated):** at run-end, DECLARE the winner; if
  right (yourself included), that traveler wins the exit-tile tiebreak. **Passive penalty:**
  if wrong, you drop to last in every tiebreak.

## Monetization (G11, dialectic-weighted — never named for what it is)

The risk verb **STAKE** and the **draft** are the monetization surfaces, because they are
already the engaging moments:

- **Fortune** (consumable) — improves the odds on a STAKE coin or a draft's rarity roll:
  the G11 "better chance of a thing." Earned through play or bought. Transparent price, no
  named category.
- **Sealed pack-draws** — theme packs (equal per mask, G7) sold as sealed draws with a
  posted chance of a **rare shadow card** (the b-cards are the desirable, showable pulls).
- **Skins** — cosmetic only; the school-yard flex.
- Each of the three contributors runs their **own theme-pack lane** (G12/G13), monetized
  independently.

Nothing above is labeled with the industry terms for it. Odds are posted (legally safe,
alongside the profile rules in `OBSCUR-DECISIONS.md`).
