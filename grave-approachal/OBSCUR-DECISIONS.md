## 1. The card model

- **Trade (final):** you choose a mask card → you get **a bonus** (usually an activated
  ability, sometimes a passive) **and a passive penalty.** Both ride on the card,
  permanently once drafted. **[R]**
  - Not a per-turn tax. Not forgoing universal verbs. The passive penalty *is* the
    handicap (G8 made literal). **[R]**
- **Two mask cards per tier (G5), split a / b:** **[R]**
  - **card a = Primary archetype** — the mask's straight coping-strategy build. Bonus +
    a modest passive penalty.
  - **card b = Shadow archetype** — **bigger bonus, bigger cost.** The inverted-tarot /
    Jungian-shadow version: the coping strategy at its dark extreme, higher risk / higher
    reward. (This is the §9 *"offhand build = a permutation."*)
- Full shape: **every mask card = bonus (activated | passive) + passive penalty;
  a = primary, b = shadow (bigger / bigger).**
- Counts, tiers, verb totals unchanged from spec: tiers 0–3, start 3 verbs (2 universal +
  1 mask), end 6; theme packs add equally to all six masks (G7). **[SPEC]**

## 2. Visibility

- **Very few cards do anything the whole board can see.** Card effects are **private by
  default.** **[R]**
- **Bonus info is useless if everyone gets it** → see-ahead / information powers reveal
  **to the owner only**, never to the table. **[R]**
- **Players do not default to seeing your X / Y / Z.** None of your state — resources,
  cards, build, information, any of it — is visible to other players by default.
  Visibility is a rare exception a specific card grants, never the norm. This is the
  single visibility principle the three bullets in this section all express. **[R]**

## 3. Game shape — Jumanji

- The die drives you forward; the game happens *to* you; **adversarial / hostile play is
  wanted, not softened.** Kids 8+ handle hostile gameplay fine — direct PvP and sabotage
  are in. **[R]**
- **Exactly one winner. Ever.** **[R]**
- **If nobody wins, everyone loses** (the House). **[R]**
- Turn order: at game start every player rolls one d6 and acts in **ascending** order —
  the only time rolling a 1 beats a 6. **[SPEC/G]**

## 4. Ending, scoring, profiles

- Game ends → **count points.** **[R]**
- **Win → your points attach to your profile.** **[R]**
- **Profiles feed leaderboards,** scoped **per map, per mask, overall,** + other
  measurables (all profile-scoped). **[R]**
- **Profiles must be legally safe.** Audience is 8+, so this means **COPPA (US, under-13)
  and GDPR-K / age-appropriate-design**: pseudonymous handles, minimal PII, collect
  nothing not strictly needed, leaderboards show handles not identities. Designed in from
  the start. **[R + legal]**

## 5. The brag-log (formerly the Chronicle)

- **WITNESS is just a brag-log, for now.** The deep Chronicle-keeper / negative-space /
  end-game reckoning is **parked.** **[R]**
- **The six mask questions ARE the per-mask brag-log headers.** Each mask's log sits under
  its question (Thorn's answers *"did you let go, or were you outrun?"*, etc.). **[R]**

## 6. Audience & tone

- **8+ means everyone — adults included.** Not a children's game adults supervise; an
  all-ages game adults choose. **[R]**
- No liminal-horror art-direction pivot. "Backrooms is popular" was *evidence that the
  audience handles adversarial/hostile play* (§3), not a theme instruction. **[R]**

## 7. Monetization

- Must be more than cosmetic: include **"better chance of a thing"** + loot-box / gacha
  *technique* (G11). **[SPEC]**
- **Do not label it "whale hooks" or "gacha"** in the design. Mechanics in, terminology
  out. **[R]**
- Each of the three contributors can **independently monetize their own contribution**
  (G12/G13). **[SPEC]**
